How to play

Small board. Big choices.

  • Take turns. Choose Would (red), Could (blue), or Should (yellow), then tap an empty square.
  • Challenge a square. To replace an enemy piece, you must have an existing piece of the same color next to it, and your color must beat the enemy's.
  • The cycle: Red beats Blue. Blue beats Yellow. Yellow beats Red.
  • Defend or counterattack. A nearby ally can make an immediate recapture possible. Defense doesn't prevent captures.
  • Win by controlling three squares in any straight row, column, or diagonal. All eight neighboring squares count as adjacent.
